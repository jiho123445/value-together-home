## Important Vercel domain setting
The production canonical origin is `https://gachi.or.kr`. In Vercel Project Settings > Domains, make `gachi.or.kr` the primary domain and keep `www.gachi.or.kr` as a redirect to the apex domain. This prevents the apex URL from returning a 308 to `www` and keeps canonical, sitemap, robots.txt, and search-console properties consistent.
